## Exact causal implementation concern

A0 source instead used `tendency=flux+local+skewpart; base=z+tendency` and reconstructed each next `z=slow+fast`. Both are algebraically equivalent but not necessarily floating-point equivalent, and SWE had already exhibited long-horizon sensitivity.
