## Why P16
P15-G3-P0/P1 authentic 32 historical retrospective GEFS–ERA5 spatial-weekly examples showed weak/static skill but **no identifiable necessity of symmetric positive-conductance information flux**. P15-G3-P2 executed a positive/negative control where a directed conservative operator achieved 8-step synthetic MSE 9.12e-6 vs symmetric flux 0.07784, but the generator used that same directed class; no real-world forecast advantage was shown. Before any neural dynamical qualification, build valid **real 6-hour forecast trajectory and matching ERA5 label** records.
